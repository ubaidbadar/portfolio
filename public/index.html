AI-powered stock research platform featuring institutional-grade market tools, insider trading trackers and prediction competitions.

  • React.js

Visit StockAlgos ↗

My role
Lead full-stack developer since 2019, working directly with the founder from architecture through deployment.
The product
A suite of research tools on live market data, including hedge-fund, Congress and insider trading trackers, an options screener and sentiment analysis, sold as per-tool subscriptions with a free trial.
AI
StockGPT is built into every tool: it reads the data on screen and lets users switch between AI models. Founder Agent chains no-code AI research pipelines across the live data sources.
In production
Live since 2019. The platform lists 10+ research tools, 11 live data sources and a monthly cash-prize competition.

Predictr.ca

Stock market prediction platform and trading competition where users compete for cash prizes and build their reputation. Available on the web, iOS and Android.

  • React.js
  • Node.js
  • Tailwind CSS
  • MongoDB

Visit Predictr ↗ Get it onGoogle Play Download on theApp Store

My role
Lead developer on the web app and the iOS and Android apps, as part of the same long-term engagement as StockAlgos.
The product
Users make daily calls on real stocks, get scored on accuracy and climb a public leaderboard. The top three share $1,000 in cash each month, and accurate predictors can build a following and monetize their posts.
Engineering
React and Tailwind CSS client with real-time updates over WebSockets and Google sign-in, on a Node.js and MongoDB backend, plus in-app purchases on mobile.
AI
AI stock summaries that draw on the StockAlgos data feeds, and in-app AI chat alongside each prediction.
In production
Live on the web, the App Store and Google Play, with 1,000+ Android installs and new releases shipping most weeks.
